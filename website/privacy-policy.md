  <h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
  <p class="updated">Effective August 29, 2026</p>

  <p>Stillhabit is built to be a quiet place. It works entirely on your device and in your own personal iCloud — we operate no servers, run no analytics, and never see your habits.</p>

  <h2>The short version</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Your habits, notes, and progress live on your device and, if iCloud sync is on, in <strong>your own</strong> iCloud account.</li>
    <li>We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, and no trackers.</li>
    <li>Purchases are handled entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment details.</li>
    <li>You can erase everything from within the app at any time.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>What is stored, and where</h2>
  <p>Habit data (titles, colors, schedules, completion history, notes, and your personal "why" lines) is saved locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, that data is stored and synced through your personal iCloud account under Apple's privacy terms. We have no access to it.</p>

  <h2>Sign in with Apple (optional)</h2>
  <p>If you choose to sign in with Apple, we store only your private, anonymized Apple identifier on your device. It is used solely to re-attach your existing subscription on a restored or new device. Your name and email are never requested or stored, and signing out or revoking access does not delete your habit data.</p>

  <h2>Purchases and subscriptions</h2>
  <p>Stillhabit Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to validate receipts and manage subscription status; this involves only an anonymized install identifier — never your identity, payment details, or habit data.</p>

  <h2>Reminders and notifications</h2>
  <p>If you set a gentle reminder, the schedule is stored locally and delivered by Apple's notification system. Notification content never leaves your device.</p>

  <h2>Face ID / app lock (optional)</h2>
  <p>The privacy curtain uses Apple's Face ID / Touch ID entirely on-device. Biometric data is handled by iOS and is never accessible to the app.</p>

  <h2>What we never do</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>No analytics or crash-reporting services receive your data.</li>
    <li>No advertising or advertising identifiers.</li>
    <li>No third-party trackers or cookies.</li>
    <li>No selling, renting, or sharing of any data — there is no data to share.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Deleting your data</h2>
  <p>Use <em>Settings → Erase All Habits &amp; Data</em> to permanently remove everything stored on the device. iCloud data can be removed through your iCloud settings. Deleting the app also removes its local data.</p>

  <h2>Children</h2>
  <p>Stillhabit contains no content or features that collect data from anyone, including children under 13.</p>

  <h2>Changes to this policy</h2>
  <p>If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.</p>

  <h2>Contact</h2>
  <p>Questions about privacy? Write to <a href="mailto:support@atkinsmedia.io">support@atkinsmedia.io</a> and a person will answer.</p>
